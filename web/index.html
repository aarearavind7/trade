How do I enable paper orders?
  1. Generate keys in your Alpaca paper account ↗.
  2. Open the project’s .env file in IntelliJ and replace ALPACA_API_KEY_ID and ALPACA_API_SECRET_KEY with your paper keys.
  3. Save, stop the launcher with Ctrl+C, and run ./start.command again. Then check the connection.
